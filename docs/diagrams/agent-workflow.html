Confidence · 1

Unverified detection

A satellite thermal anomaly only. Never called a confirmed wildfire.

Confidence · 2

Corroborated signal

A detection plus a compatible second source or calculation.

Confidence · 3

Officially reported

Matched to an official incident or perimeter source.

Confidence · 4

Insufficient evidence

Data is missing, stale, incompatible or contradictory.
